FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have compiled answers to the most common dental queries we receive.

How often should I visit a dentist?

We recommend scheduling a routine dental examination and professional cleaning every 6 months. Regular checkups help diagnose plaque buildup, gum diseases, and early decay before they develop into serious, costly conditions.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, professional teeth whitening is completely safe when performed under dental supervision. Our whitening gels are formulated to minimize tooth sensitivity while protecting your enamel, giving you bright results without risk of structural damage.

Do you provide emergency treatment?

Yes, we prioritize dental emergencies. If you have severe toothache, a broken tooth, swelling, or a lost restoration, call our clinic immediately. We reserve daily emergency slots to provide pain relief and treatments on the same day.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely! We offer pediatric dental care for children of all ages. Our friendly team uses gentle methods to keep children comfortable, teaching them healthy brushing practices while monitoring their dental alignment and growth.

What should I do during a dental emergency?

For a knocked-out tooth, rinse it gently in water without scrubbing the root, place it back in the socket or keep it moist in a glass of milk, and visit us within 1 hour. For severe tooth pain or swelling, rinse with warm salt water and apply a cold compress to your face. Call us immediately.
